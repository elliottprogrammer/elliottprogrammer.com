About Me

👋 Hey there! I’m Bryan Elliott

I’m a Frontend Engineer with 15+ years of experience building sleek, scalable, and lightning-fast web apps. I speak fluent JavaScript, dream in React (and Next.js), and have a long-standing love affair with TypeScript. Clean code, intuitive UX, and pixel-perfect UI’s? Yes please! 😃

For the past 5 years, I was crafting the web at Automattic, working on high-impact projects for WordPress.com and Jetpack. I led complex initiatives like revamping the checkout system used by millions, all while collaborating remotely with brilliant minds across the globe.

But I’m not just about the code. I’m a mentor, team player, and process improver, always looking for ways to make engineering more fun, efficient, and human. I’m also super excited about the future of AI-assisted development. Tools like ChatGPT and Copilot are a natural extension of my workflow.

When I’m not coding, you’ll probably find me out fishing in the Florida sun 🎣 — chasing a big Snook, Tarpon, or Jack Crevalle (true story: I once made the cover of Waterline Magazine with a 15-pounder 💪).

I believe great products are built by curious people who care. Care about quality, about users, and about the team. If that sounds like you too, let’s connect!
